# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 7, 2026_

This policy explains what **Megsy LLC** ("Megsy", "we") collects when you use Ceyo, why, and the choices you have.

## 1. What we collect
- **Account:** your email address, display name, age, profile photo and how you plan to use the app (from onboarding).
- **Your content:** bots you create and their instructions, groups, chats, files you upload, memories you ask bots to keep, routines and voice messages.
- **Settings:** language, theme and notification preferences.
- **Connected apps:** when you connect an app, we store a reference to that connection so your bots can act for you. We do not store your passwords for those apps.
- **Technical data:** basic information needed to run and secure the Service, such as device type and error logs.

Voice recordings you make are stored on your own device; only the text and the audio needed to answer are sent for processing.

## 2. How we use it
- to provide the Service: run your bots, answer your messages, voice and calls, and complete tasks you approve;
- to keep your account secure and prevent abuse;
- to provide support and communicate with you about your account;
- to meet legal obligations.

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do not use your private chats for advertising.

## 3. Who processes it
To provide the Service we send only the data needed to trusted service providers acting for us, including:
- hosting and database providers;
- AI model providers that generate replies;
- voice providers for speech and transcription;
- web browsing providers when a bot visits a site for you;
- integration providers for apps you choose to connect;
- email delivery for sign-in codes.

We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users and the public.

## 4. How long we keep it
We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete a bot, chat, memory or file it is removed from your account. When you delete your account, we delete your data within 30 days, except where we must keep it longer by law.

## 5. Security
Your data is private to your account and protected by access rules on our servers. Service keys are stored only on the server. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your email account safe.

## 6. Your rights
Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, or object to certain uses. Email us and we will respond within 30 days.

## 7. Children
The Service is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. International transfers
Our providers may process data in other countries, including the United States. We use providers that protect data with appropriate safeguards.

## 9. Changes
If we change this policy we will update the date above and notify you in the app when the change is significant.
