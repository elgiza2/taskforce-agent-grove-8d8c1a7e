# Cookies & Local Storage

_Last updated: November 7, 2026_

This notice explains browser storage used by Ceyo, operated by **Megsy LLC**. It supplements the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## Sign-in & essential storage
The browser stores sign-in session information so you can remain signed in and renew your session. Hosted services may also use cookies necessary to deliver and secure requests. Blocking all essential storage can prevent sign-in or account features from working.

## Preferences & device-local data
Local storage may retain language, theme, device preferences and account-scoped Vault labels. Voice recordings are cached in IndexedDB on the sending device. Some settings are also persisted with your account. Clearing browser data may remove local labels or recordings and will not necessarily remove server-side history.

## Diagnostics & third-party services
Application requests can generate operational logs and technical diagnostics. External services and sites you visit apply their own storage policies. This page does not claim that every provider's storage has been audited or that a comprehensive third-party-cookie consent system is active.

## Your choices
Use the available app settings to change preferences and sign out. Browser controls let you remove cookies, site storage and IndexedDB data or restrict future storage. Where consent is legally required for optional tracking, that tracking must not be treated as authorized simply because you use the Service.
