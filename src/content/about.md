# About Ceyo

Ceyo is an AI workspace owned and operated by **Megsy LLC**.

## A workspace for your agents
Create personal bots with their own instructions, organize them into groups and keep conversations, saved memories and supported documents together. Your primary bot and specialists share your account's workspace rather than separate copies of your information.

## Tools with clear boundaries
Available providers support AI replies, web-page reading, voice processing and authorized app integrations. Important output still needs human review. A bot saying it can perform a task does not establish that computer control, code execution or a connected account is available.

## You remain in control
Review requests to change data in connected services before approving them. Manage your bots, memories and authorized connections through the available account controls. AI can make mistakes; Ceyo is not a replacement for professional advice or emergency services.
