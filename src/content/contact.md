# Contact

Contact **Megsy LLC**, the company operating Ceyo, at [CEO@megsyai.com](mailto:CEO@megsyai.com).

## Product support
Tell us what happened, the page involved and the approximate time. A screenshot with personal information hidden can help. Do not include a password, sign-in code or session token.

## Billing & subscriptions
Include the purchase date, payment provider and receipt or transaction reference. Do not send full card numbers, security codes, wallet private keys or recovery phrases. See [Subscription Terms](/subscription-terms).

## Privacy & account requests
Identify the request: access, correction, export, deletion or a question about processing. Use the account's email where possible. We may need to verify ownership before releasing information or changing an account. See [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## Security reports
Describe the affected page and reproducible steps without exposing another person's data. Do not post vulnerabilities or credentials in public chats. See [Security](/security).

## Business & legal enquiries
Send company and legal correspondence to the same contact address, with a clear subject. No separate office address or response-time guarantee is published on this page.
